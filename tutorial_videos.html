Making guide

Two ways to make your plushie

Choose the method that matches the material you want to use.

Method 1

Felt plushie

No turning inside out
Running stitch
Attach one felt piece directly onto another felt piece.
Blanket stitch
Sew visibly around the outside edge of the plushie.

Method 2

Fabric plushie

Turn the plushie inside out

All three fabric plushies use running stitch and hidden stitch.

Running stitch - before turning
Place the two fabric pieces back-to-back and sew them together.
Hidden stitch - after turning
Close the small opening that was left for turning and stuffing.
Whip stitch - bottom example only
Attach the extra ear to the main plushie body.
